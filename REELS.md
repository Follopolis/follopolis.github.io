# First reels and Shorts

Both videos are 1080×1920, silent, in the `brand` folder. Post the **same file** as an Instagram Reel and a YouTube Short.

| File | Length | Post it |
|---|---|---|
| `brand/follopolis-reel-day1.mp4` | 16 s | **First.** Today. |
| `brand/follopolis-reel-preview.mp4` | 18 s | Second. Tomorrow, or 3+ hours after the first. |

**Add a sound in the app.** Both apps let you pick a trending sound when posting. Reels with a trending sound get shown to more people. Keep the volume low so the text is the focus.
